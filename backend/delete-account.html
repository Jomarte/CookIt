Delete Your Account & Data

This page explains how to permanently delete your CookIt account and all associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly inside the CookIt app at any time. No email or form is required.

  1. 1
    Open the CookIt app on your device.
  2. 2
    Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. 3
    Tap the Settings icon (⚙) in the top-right corner of your profile.
  4. 4
    Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  5. 5
    Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears. Your account and all data will be permanently erased immediately.
⚠️ This action is irreversible. Once confirmed, your account cannot be recovered.

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your CookIt account, the following data is permanently and immediately removed from our servers:

Data Type Status
Profile information (name, username, email, avatar, bio, nationality) Deleted immediately
All recipes you created (including ingredients, steps, and images) Deleted immediately
Saved recipes and cooked history Deleted immediately
Likes, ratings, and comments Deleted immediately
Followers and following relationships Deleted immediately
Shopping list Deleted immediately
Notifications and authentication tokens Deleted immediately

We do not retain any personal data after account deletion. No backup copies are kept.

Need Help?

If you are unable to access the app or need assistance with account deletion, please contact us at jmgpcl@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request — CookIt" and we will process your request within 7 days.
